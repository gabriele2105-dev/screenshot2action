Data controller and scope

Carlotta Borsci is the data controller for Screenshot2Action and its information website. For privacy requests, email developerlotty@gmail.com.

Screenshot2Action is intended for people aged 18 and over. No date of birth, account or identification is required to use its on-device features.

Screenshots, notes, reminders and messages

The app opens an image you select with Android’s picker or share from another app. It displays the image alongside the form you fill in. It does not use OCR or require general access to your image library.

When you save an item, the private on-device archive stores what is needed for that action, such as note text, reminder details or an image attachment. App preferences are stored on-device. Android may ask for permission to deliver reminders. The developer does not receive this content or provide a server to synchronize it. Local features are provided at your request under Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR, where applicable.

If you open your calendar or notes app, Android passes the details you entered for that action to the receiving app. If you share an image or use Ask AI, the receiving app can access the image and optional text. If you use Write a message, the messaging app you pick receives only the text. You choose the recipient and confirm sending in that app. The receiving service may store or synchronize data under its own settings and policy.

Advertising and consent choices

Ad-free builds omit advertising SDKs. Ad-supported builds use Google AdMob via Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). The free edition may show a banner at the bottom of app screens. Screenshot2Action does not pass screenshots, note or message text, or reminder details to these SDKs.

According to Google, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or app identifiers. UMP handles and stores signals about privacy choices. Purposes may include serving, measuring and analysing ads, personalization where permitted, security, and preventing fraud and abuse. The consent form lists the applicable purposes and partners.

Where required, we ask for consent for personalized advertising and for non-essential storage of, or access to, information on your device under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR and Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code, where applicable. Strictly necessary security and abuse-prevention activities may rely on a legitimate interest in protecting the service where permitted and subject to device-access rules.

You may decline and continue using the app. Declining excludes the purposes for which you withheld consent. Google may make limited ads available based on your choices, the advertising configuration and applicable rules. Where the form applies, you can change your choices in Settings → Privacy and advertising → Consent preferences. Withdrawal takes effect going forward and does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps and the Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Website and support

Screenshot2Action’s information pages contain no ads or site analytics installed by us. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Pages data collection notice and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

If you email support, we receive your address, message and attachments through Gmail. We use them to respond under Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR where applicable, or under Article 6(1)(f) based on our legitimate interest in handling other correspondence. Article 6(1)(c) applies where a legal duty requires a response or retention. Contacting support is optional.

Recipients and transfers

Depending on the features used, recipients may include the apps you select, GitHub for hosting, Google for Gmail and, in ad-supported builds, Google and the ad partners shown in the consent form. Authorities may receive data where legally required.

Providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Applicable safeguards vary by service and country and may include an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. Contact the controller to learn more or request a copy of the applicable safeguards. Google and GitHub describe international transfers in their privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Saved items remain on your device until you delete them from the Archive. To clear local content and preferences, use Android Settings → Apps → Screenshot2Action → Storage → Clear data or uninstall the app. Use the relevant service’s controls for copies held by other apps.

Support correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle the request. Longer retention is limited to legal obligations or establishing, exercising or defending a legal claim. Google and GitHub describe their retention criteria in their policies. UMP manages the validity of advertising preferences and may ask for them again when necessary.

Your rights

Email developerlotty@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where GDPR conditions apply. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority, such as the Italian Data Protection Authority.

Use the same contact if you believe we received a minor’s personal data. Updates will appear here with a new version and date.
